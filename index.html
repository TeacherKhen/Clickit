Just Click It!
A fun word-clicking game for young learners 🎉

HOW TO PLAY

  • Listen carefully to the teacher's target word.
  • Find and touch the correct word box as fast as you can.
  • Your selection will appear in your answer area.
  • In Fastest Player Mode, tap 🔒 LOCK ANSWER when you are ready.
  • In Timer Mode, simply select your answer before the timer reaches zero.
  • Give a point to the player/team with a correct answer.

1, 2, or 3 Players: Be quick, be fair, and have fun! 🌟
